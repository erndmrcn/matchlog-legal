Privacy Policy for MatchLog

Effective Date: April 23, 2026
Last Updated: April 23, 2026

MatchLog ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application that allows users to browse football matches, react to matches, and keep a personal match history.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

a. Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • your email address
  • your display name
  • your sign-in provider identifier (for example, Apple Sign-In identifier), if applicable

b. User Content

When you use MatchLog, we may collect content you create in the app, such as:

  • match reactions
  • ratings
  • watched status
  • notes or comments you save

c. Technical and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical information to operate and improve the app, such as:

  • app version
  • device type
  • operating system version
  • crash logs
  • diagnostic information
  • analytics events related to app usage

d. Match and Content Data

We display football-related match data obtained from third-party data providers. This data is not personal data by itself, but your interaction with it inside the app may be associated with your account if you are signed in.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate MatchLog
  • save your match history, reactions, and notes
  • sync your data across sessions or devices, if supported
  • improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
  • detect bugs, abuse, fraud, or misuse
  • respond to support requests
  • comply with legal obligations

3. Legal Bases (if applicable)

If you are located in a region where legal bases are required, we process personal information based on:

  • performance of a contract (providing the app and requested features)
  • legitimate interests (app security, maintenance, and improvement)
  • your consent, where required
  • compliance with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following cases:

  • with service providers that help us operate the app (such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, or support tools)
  • with data infrastructure providers as needed to store or process app data
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or the public
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets

5. Third-Party Services

MatchLog may rely on third-party services to function. Depending on your implementation, these may include:

  • Apple Sign In
  • analytics providers
  • crash reporting providers
  • backend hosting providers
  • football data providers

These third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • provide the service
  • maintain your account
  • comply with legal obligations
  • resolve disputes
  • enforce agreements

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, unless we are legally required to retain certain information.

7. Account Deletion

If MatchLog offers account creation, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data from within the app or by contacting us at the email below, unless retention is legally required.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • access your personal data
  • correct inaccurate personal data
  • request deletion of your personal data
  • object to or restrict certain processing
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at: matchlog.support@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

MatchLog is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required by applicable law in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Data Transfers

If you use MatchLog from outside the country where our servers or service providers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries that may have different data protection laws.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date and may provide additional notice where required.
