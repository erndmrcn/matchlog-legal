Frequently Asked Questions

What is MatchLog?

MatchLog helps users browse football matches, react to them, and keep a personal history of watched matches and activity.

How do I contact support?

Send an email to matchlog.support@gmail.com. Please include any useful context such as your device, app version, and a short description of the issue.

How do I request account or data deletion?

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing matchlog.support@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account.

What should I include in a deletion request?

Please mention that you want your MatchLog account and personal data deleted, and include the email address associated with your account so we can locate it safely.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

You can review them here: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

If you want to delete your account, remove associated personal data, or ask a privacy-related question, contact matchlog.support@gmail.com.

For faster handling, send the request from the email address associated with your MatchLog account and clearly state whether you are requesting account deletion, personal data deletion, or general privacy support.
